## Lessons Learned

- [SKILL: serde]: Confirmed that #[serde(rename_all = "camelCase")] does NOT handle acronyms correctly. GetSWUpdateInfo would serialize as "getSwUpdateInfo" not "getSWUpdateInfo". Must use explicit #[serde(rename = "...")] for variants with acronyms.
- [SKILL: clap]: Making `command: Option<Commands>` works well for optional completions mode. Shell completions can be handled before requiring a subcommand.
- [PLAN]: Phase 3 (Host consolidation) was mostly completed during Phase 1 - the import change and Display impl were needed to make SonyError work. Plan dependency ordering was slightly off.

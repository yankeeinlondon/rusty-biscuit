## Lessons Learned

- [RUST-2024]: `std::env::set_var` and `remove_var` now require `unsafe` blocks in Rust 2024 edition
- [AXUM]: Axum does not normalize trailing slashes - `/path` and `/path/` are different routes
- [PATTERN]: Clone-mutate-save pattern is essential for RwLock with disk persistence to avoid deadlocks
- [TOKIO]: Must use `tokio::sync::RwLock` not `std::sync::RwLock` in async handlers

**Agent:** `orchestrator` | **Skills:** biscuit-terminal, rust | **Complexity:** Low
**Deps:** None | **Parallel:** No

**Goal:** Add `<bg-rgb X,Y,Z>text</bg-rgb>` block tag support for background RGB colors.

**Deliver:**

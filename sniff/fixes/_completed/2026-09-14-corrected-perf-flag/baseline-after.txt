## Definition-of-Success items visible above

1. Hierarchical, unit-aligned timing tree with a synthetic `Total` root,
   detection-domain re-parenting (`filesystem.*` below `detect.filesystem`),
   generic dotted nesting (`classify.fallback`), duration-descending sibling
   order, `×N` call counts, and exactly one `▇ HOT` row.
2. Counters render as a separate `Counters` tree; no counter appears in the
   timing tree.
3. The single HOT row is `shared_walk → docs` — the greatest measured
   non-root stage, exactly as ruling R-4 predicted.
5. `--plain` output holds no escape sequence; connector and marker glyphs
   still follow the detected terminal, not the `--plain` flag.

## Structural facts that bear on the Phase 1 rulings

- **R-2 is live.** `filesystem.shared_walk` is measured (241.48 ms) *and* is a
  prefix of `filesystem.shared_walk.docs` (1483.78 ms). A measured node that is
  also a parent is not hypothetical on this host.
- **R-2 second case is live.** `filesystem.file_inventory`,
  `filesystem.file_inventory.classify`, and `filesystem.file_inventory.walk`
  are all unmeasured synthetic intermediates.
- **R-3 holds on a full run.** `detect.os`, `detect.hardware`, `detect.network`,
  and `detect.filesystem` are all present, so every `os.*`, `hardware.*`,
  `network.*`, and `filesystem.*` key re-parents. No stage is literally named
  `detect.<domain>.<rest>`, so re-parenting collides with nothing.
- **R-3's absent-`detect.<domain>` branch is also live.** `sniff os --perf`
  emits `detect.os` but no `detect.filesystem`/`detect.network`/
  `detect.hardware`, and focused non-detection commands emit total-only
  reports.
- **Shares legitimately exceed 1.0.** `filesystem.shared_walk.docs` accumulates
  1483.78 ms against a 722.71 ms wall clock (4286 repeated calls). The
  projection must not clamp; `MetricShare::Of` caps *display* at 99%.
- **HOT will land on a nested leaf,** not on a domain: the greatest measured
  non-root total is `filesystem.shared_walk.docs`.
- **No counter key collides with a stage key,** but the near-miss the spec
  anticipated is real: stage `…classify.extension` vs counter
  `…classified_extension`. Two separate trees (R3) keeps them apart.

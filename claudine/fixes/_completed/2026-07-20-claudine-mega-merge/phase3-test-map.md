the integration worktree. Tests named below are present at frozen proxy
revision `e348486c810969abe87a6b7209979034f5454b07` unless identified as
foundation or merged-tree structural evidence.

cycle. Overlay state is deliberately non-persistent; its tests instead reread
the target across retry, resume, and loop refresh and assert source and target
bytes remain unchanged.

Per the review rubric, Level 1 generated bytes are necessary but not enough to mark it production-ready. Existing Level 2 prose tests prove generic Prose color/style capture through WezTerm/Kitty, but they do not render a `StatusBlock` with body plus hint, do not verify the hint line specifically, and do not exercise the custom-border fallback.
- Fix direction: add a Level 2 test that renders a real `StatusBlock` body plus hint inside tmux, WezTerm, or Kitty and captures the pane. It should assert the plain captured text has the leading quoted blank line, body, separator, and quoted hint, and the raw captured output has italic SGR on the hint line. Add a custom-border variant or at least a Level 1 raw-SGR assertion for the bespoke path, since that path bypasses the shared render tree.

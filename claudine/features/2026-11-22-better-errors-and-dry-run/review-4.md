**Class.** The check uses the filesystem before root initialization to decide an outcome that depends on the filesystem after initialization.

**Invariant.** Changing `ensure_file: ["gen/notes.md", "Notes"]` to `ensure_file: ["{{ target }}", "Notes"]` with `target: gen/notes.md` should preserve the dependent verdict.
